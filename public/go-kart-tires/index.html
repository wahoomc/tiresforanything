All tires / Go-karts & karting
Sprint · cadet · rental karts

Go-kart tires

In karting, the tire often comes down to your class rules. Start with what your sanctioning body allows, then pick the compound for your track and budget. Racing a car instead? See race car tires.

Check your class rules first

Most racing classes specify approved tires, sizes or treadwear ratings. A tire that's faster but not legal for your class won't do you any good.

Karting

Sprint kart sizes
10x4.50-5

A common front size. Rears are wider, like 11x7.10-5, both on 5 in wheels.

Compounds

Hard to soft

Harder compounds last longer and suit practice and spec classes. Softer compounds grip harder but wear out fast. Rain tires have their own tread and compound.

Good to know

  • Heat cycles age race tires. Each time they heat up and cool down, they lose a little grip, even with tread left.
  • Competition-only tires aren't street legal. If the sidewall says "for competition use only," it stays on the trailer.
  • Store them cool and out of the sun, away from electric motors that give off ozone.
